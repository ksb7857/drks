About Ket

Holistic care, grounded in evidence

Ket sees children of all ages for general paediatric concerns, and has special interests in allergy and ADHD.

Regardless of the reason for referral, she takes a thorough approach to evaluating all aspects of health and development, including preventative health.

She has a personal understanding of living with food allergies, eczema and hayfever in her family, which informs her empathetic and practical approach.

Outside of work, Ket is a mother of two and enjoys reading, painting and sewing.

Background

Ket has over 14 years of experience in paediatric medicine. Prior to her current roles, she spent six years as a Staff Specialist at the Children's Hospital at Westmead and National Centre for Immunisation Research and Surveillance (NCIRS), where she provided scientific and technical support to the Australian Technical Advisory Group on Immunisation (ATAGI) and contributed to clinical guideline development, particularly for the Australian Immunisation Handbook. She has contributed to multiple peer-reviewed publications.

This experience has shaped how Ket approaches difficult clinical questions, interpreting evolving research to support genuinely collaborative conversations with families.
