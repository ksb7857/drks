About Ket

Family-centred care, grounded in evidence

Ket sees children of all ages for general paediatric concerns, and has particular expertise in allergy (including IgE-mediated food allergy, cow's milk allergy, eczema and hayfever) as well as neurodevelopmental paediatrics, particularly ADHD assessment and management, with a neuro-affirming approach.

She conducts skin prick testing on-site and can coordinate food challenges and aeroallergen immunotherapy where indicated. She is passionate about providing evidence-based care within a holistic framework that takes the whole child and family into account.

Ket has a personal understanding of living with food allergies, eczema and hayfever in the family, which informs her empathetic and practical approach to supporting families navigating these challenges.

She holds a Conjoint Clinical Lecturer appointment at the University of Sydney and has an background in research and clinical guideline development, including six years as a Staff Specialist at the Children's Hospital at Westmead and at the National Centre for Immunisation Research and Surveillance (NCIRS).
