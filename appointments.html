Telehealth · Australia-wide

Cub Care — ADHD & Neurodevelopment

For families outside of Sydney who are unable to travel for an initial ADHD assessment, Ket offers appointments via Cub Care's Behaviour, Attention and Hyperactivity Clinic. Consultations are conducted by video in a shared-care model with your local GP.

A GP referral is required, and should include your child's height, weight, blood pressure and heart rate.
